# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-18

First independent Microbial Empires playable slice.

Added the ecological simulation, five cell classes, two asymmetric communities, five habitat objectives, original procedural microscopy graphics, group controls, ecological policy controls, autonomous opposing community, local saves, offline build, field guide, scientific notes and microscope inspection.

Pre-release checks corrected unintended idle expansion, route-cache inconsistency after restoring saves, obstacle-boundary routing, costly per-frame hunting path searches, and victory interruption by insignificant incursions. The AI now responds when the opponent begins a winning hold.

The first release intentionally leaves the full evolution tree, fog of war, multiplayer, advanced squads and calibrated microbiology for later milestones. Match pacing, stalled mirror matches and actual school hardware performance still need work; see VALIDATION.md.
