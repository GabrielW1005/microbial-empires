QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Select & command

Drag a box around green-marked cells. Right-click a habitat to move. On a touchpad, select Move, then click your destination.

02

Bring a food web

Send algae, recyclers and predators together. Algae use light; bacteria use organic matter. Ciliates eat bacteria. Amoebae engulf smaller cells.

03

Hold the network

Occupy a dotted habitat for 32 seconds to capture it. Hold three uncontested habitats for 90 seconds. A balanced contest or stronger rival presence interrupts the victory timer.

    BIOLOGY & FIELD NOTES

    Real ideas. A model world.

    Matter moves. Energy is spent.

    Algae consume 0.8 inorganic carbon + 0.2 mineral units per new unit of biomass, using light. Recyclers break detritus down and grow on dissolved organic matter. Grazers retain at most 65% of prey matter; the rest becomes detritus. Respired matter returns to inorganic pools. Division partitions existing biomass and stored energy between daughters.

    These coefficients are deliberately simplified bookkeeping, not calibrated biological stoichiometry. Oxygen is a separate bounded environmental index. Atmospheric exchange and photosynthesis replenish it; aerobic metabolism lowers it.

    Different organisms, different strategies.

    Ciliates here are specialized bacterial grazers. Amoebae surround smaller compatible prey with pseudopodia. Their food vacuoles remain visible during digestion. Matrix builders invest biomass in extracellular material that protects nearby cells and delays feeding access.

    Explicit game abstractions

    Factions, capture rings, controllable feeding policies, population ceilings and strategic commands are invented. Real microbes do not recognize military allies. Minutes represent compressed ecological time. These are functional analogues, not exact species simulations. Bacteria remain smaller than protists, but size ratios are compressed for usability. Amoeba movement represents crawling along a thin submerged substrate.

    Carbon, nutrients and organic matter are conserved inside the model after initialization. Energy is an uncalibrated physiological index; heat is not simulated. Light, oxygen and chemical fields are only colored when a labeled visualization overlay is enabled. Motion uses immediate drag-dominated steering; there is no fluid solver, ballistic coasting or molecular membrane simulation.

    First-version scope

    One map, one playable Sunlit community and a Sediment AI opponent. No fog of war, multiplayer, evolutionary research tree or calibrated physical units yet. No runtime external assets, advertising, accounts or student data collection. Saves stay in this browser under Microbial Empires' own storage keys.
