    How to play

    1. Gather. Choose a Collector, then right-click a glowing resource deposit. The cursor identifies the resource. Collectors haul their loads back to a friendly Core or Depot, then return to gather more.
    2. Build and train. Select a Collector to place a Foundry, Depot, Tower or Shelter. Your Core trains Collectors and Scouts. A Foundry trains Guards, Sparks and Wardens. A Shelter increases your unit limit.
    3. Scout and attack. Use the minimap to find the rival colony. Select combat units and right-click opponents, or choose Attack and click the ground to fight while advancing.
